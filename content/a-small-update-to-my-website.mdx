I have been working on a few updates to thehomelabber.io.

The website now has a [Knowledge wiki](/knowledge). This is a place where I can collect practical notes, instructions, and solutions from my homelab. It will grow over time as I learn new things and document my setups.

I also added categories and a search bar to both the [blog](/blog) and the wiki. This makes it easier to find articles about a specific topic, such as Proxmox, Linux, or networking.

Building these features was also a learning experience. Step by step, I learned more about how the website reads MDX files, shows articles, and filters content.

There is still more I want to improve, but I am happy with this update. My website is slowly becoming a place where I can share my experiences and keep useful information together.
