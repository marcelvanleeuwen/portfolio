Quickly Check Your Public IP💡

If you’re hosting services at home like Home Assistant, a media server, or a private cloud it’s often useful to know your current public IP address. Instead of logging into your router or using a browser, just run this in your terminal:

```bash
curl https://ipinfo.io/ip
```
