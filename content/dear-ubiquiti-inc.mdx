Dear Ubiquiti Inc.,

We need to have a quick word.

I’m running a homelab and using your gear daily, and I really like it. But I’m missing one thing: a server rack that is higher than 12U for home use.

42U? That’s just too high for most homelabs. It quickly becomes overkill in a home setup. What would actually hit the sweet spot is something in between a taller home focused rack that still keeps a clean, premium look.

Also: 60cm depth would be perfect. Not too deep, fits nicely in a home environment, and still gives enough room for proper gear and cable management.

For homelab users like me, that balance between size, usability, and aesthetics is exactly what’s missing right now.

How about creating a bigger rack for home users?

Who’s with me? Let’s do this!
