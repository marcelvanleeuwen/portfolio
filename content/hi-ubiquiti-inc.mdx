# Hi Ubiquiti Inc.,

I think I may have found a small hint inside Ubiquiti Design Center about a possible new 24U rack from Ubiquiti and honestly, I really hope it becomes real soon.

Right now, my current rack is simply too small for my growing homelab and networking setup. Over the last few years, the UniFi ecosystem has grown a lot, and many of us are running more equipment than ever before: gateways, switches, patch panels, NAS systems, UPS units, and mini servers for virtualization.

One thing I really like about the UniFi racks is the premium look and clean design. The white finish, and matching UniFi hardware make setups look incredibly clean compared to many traditional black server racks.

Personally, I would love to see a version around 60x60cm. That depth would be perfect for networking gear, patch panels, lightweight servers, and compact storage systems without taking up too much space in a room.

At the moment this is still speculation, but the hints inside the Design Center definitely caught my attention. If a 24U UniFi rack really is coming, I think there will be a lot of interest from the community.

Now we wait
