# My Homelab Through the Years

My homelab has changed quite a bit over the years. What started with an old desktop PC grew into several different rack setups. It runs the services I use every day, but it is also where I learn and try new things.

## The racks in the shed

The photos from **2009**, **2011**, and **2017** show some of my older setups. In 2009, I had a smaller rack with a screen and keyboard on top. The 2011 version had a taller cabinet with more room inside. By 2017, the rack held more equipment and a lot more cables.

I had several versions of my racks in the shed, but that was not always a success. Heat was the main problem. In summer, the temperature in the shed could reach **30°C**. The servers added even more heat to a room that was already too warm.

I had a large extractor fan installed to pull the hot air out. It helped, but it was not enough on hot days. Keeping everything cool was a problem I kept coming back to.

## Moving into the house

When energy prices rose sharply in Europe, I decided to change my setup. I moved from larger servers to Minisforum mini PCs to use less electricity. This was also a good reason to move the racks from the shed into the hallway of my home.

The **2024** photo shows the smaller setup in the house. By **2026**, it had become the two-rack setup I use today. The layout has changed, but the idea is still the same: run the services I need without using more power than necessary.

Moving indoors has worked well. The heat from the racks now helps warm the house a little, which is a nice bonus. Since the move, I have had no more heat problems in my racks.

## My setup today

Today, I use Minisforum systems running Proxmox, with UniFi equipment for networking and storage. I keep the full equipment list on the [hardware page](/hardware).

This is still a production homelab. It runs my daily services and [FluxNodes](/flux), so keeping it working matters to me. I also use it to learn, test changes, and work on new projects. I share updates on my [blog](/blog) and keep practical notes in the [wiki](/knowledge).
