I am Marcel van Leeuwen, a homelab enthusiast from Ede in the Netherlands. I have enjoyed working with computers and networks since I was young. Most of my time at home goes into Linux, networking, and running my own servers.

My homelab runs on Proxmox, with virtual machines and containers for my applications. This is a production setup that runs the services I use every day, as well as a place to learn and test new things. I also use Docker and am learning Kubernetes. I like building things, finding out why something does not work, and learning as I go. You can read about my setup on the [homelab page](/homelab) and see the equipment I use on the [hardware page](/hardware).

I have been running FluxNodes for several years. Flux is a cloud network made up of servers run by people around the world. These servers provide processing power, memory, and storage for hosting applications. My FluxNodes are part of this network and run in production. They need to stay online, so keeping them running and up to date is part of managing my homelab.

I tried to turn my IT hobby into a job. In April and May 2025, I did an internship in the Infrastructure and Management department at RotaForm in Lelystad. I enjoyed the work and being part of the team. I have not managed to make the move into an IT job. I think my age may have played a part, but I still enjoy working with IT and learning new things.

I also work with AI and use it to help me learn, write code, and work on projects like this website. I enjoy trying different tools and finding out where they are useful. I believe AI has a big future and will change a lot in our daily lives and at work, from how we find information to how we solve problems and do everyday tasks. I want to keep learning about it and see how I can use it in my own work.

I use this website to write down what I learn and share it with others. My [blog](/blog) has updates about my homelab, IT, and everyday life. The [wiki](/knowledge) has practical notes and step-by-step guides that I can return to when I need them. I hope they are useful to you too.
